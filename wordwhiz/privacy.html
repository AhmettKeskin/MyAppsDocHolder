Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to WordWhiz. This Privacy Policy explains what information the WordWhiz mobile app collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. WordWhiz does not require an account and does not ask for your name or email address.

Information We Collect

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

WordWhiz stores the following information on your device only:

  • Game progress (current level, completed levels, daily puzzle progress)
  • Daily puzzle streak
  • Language preference
  • Coin and hint balances
  • Settings (for example haptics and notification preferences)

This information is not uploaded to our servers. It is removed when you delete the app.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, app version, language and region settings, and app or device identifiers
  • Usage data: app sessions, levels played and completed, hints used, daily puzzles completed, screens viewed, and interactions with ads and the store
  • Diagnostics: crash reports and performance information
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA): only if you allow tracking when iOS asks you

Purchase Information

Payments are processed by Apple. We never receive your card or payment details. When you make a purchase, we receive the product, price, currency and a random customer ID that identifies the purchase (not you personally). This purchase record is stored by RevenueCat and in our Firebase database so we can unlock what you bought, restore purchases and detect fraud.

Third-Party Services

Google Firebase

  • Firebase Analytics: app usage statistics and in-app events (for example level completion, hint use, purchases and ad impressions, including estimated ad revenue)
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports and diagnostic information
  • Firebase Remote Config: lets us adjust game settings (for example rewards and ad frequency) without an app update
  • Firebase Realtime Database: stores the purchase records described above

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect your advertising identifier (if you allow tracking), IP address, device information and ad interaction data. Depending on your choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases and subscriptions. RevenueCat receives purchase information from Apple together with a random app user ID. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Consent and Tracking Choices

  • Consent prompt: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or in another region where it is required, WordWhiz shows a consent form provided by Google before personalized ads are shown. You can accept, reject or choose specific purposes.
  • App Tracking Transparency: iOS asks whether WordWhiz may track you across apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalized based on tracking.
  • Changing your choices: you can review or withdraw your consent at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy Choices, and change tracking permission in iOS under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, WordWhiz schedules reminders (for example for the daily puzzle) on your device. These notifications are created locally; no personal information is sent to a server to deliver them. You can turn them off in the app settings or in iOS Settings.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the game and keep your progress
  • To deliver and restore in-app purchases
  • To show ads, and personalized ads only where you have allowed it
  • To understand how the game is played and improve it
  • To fix crashes and technical issues
  • To tune game settings such as rewards and ad frequency
  • To answer you if you contact us

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • With service providers: Google (Firebase and AdMob) and RevenueCat, as described above
  • For legal reasons: if required by law or to protect our rights
  • In a business transfer: in case of a merger, sale or acquisition

Data Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with our service providers. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

WordWhiz does not require registration and does not ask for names or email addresses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. To:

  • Withdraw ad consent: use Settings > Privacy Choices in the app
  • Stop tracking: use iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking
  • Delete local data: delete the app
  • Request access to or deletion of other data: contact us through the support form below

You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Data Retention

  • Local data: stays on your device until you delete the app
  • Analytics, crash and advertising data: kept by Google according to its retention settings and policies
  • Purchase records: kept as long as needed to provide and restore your purchases and to meet legal obligations

International Data Transfers

Our service providers may process your information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, these transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date at the top when we do. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Third-Party Links

WordWhiz may contain links to third-party websites or services (such as ads). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties.
