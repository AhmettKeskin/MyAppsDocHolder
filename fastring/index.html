FastRing Support

FastRing is a local-first intermittent fasting timer for iPhone, Apple Watch, widgets, complications, and Live Activities.

Frequently asked questions

Will the timer remain accurate if the app is closed?

Yes. FastRing saves timestamps instead of relying on a background countdown. The displayed time is recalculated whenever the app or a system surface refreshes.

Why did I not receive a completion alert?

Open FastRing Settings and enable Fast completion. Also confirm notifications are allowed in iOS Settings. Delivery timing is controlled by the operating system.

How do I correct an accidental session?

Open History, tap the session, then edit its start, end, or status. Sessions cannot overlap.

Does FastRing use Apple Health?

No. The first release does not request HealthKit access.

How do I delete or export my data?

Open Settings in FastRing. Use Export JSON to make a copy or Delete all local data to remove fasting and hydration records from the device.
