PulseTogether Privacy Policy

Effective: August 11, 2026

PulseTogether is a private communication app for exactly two connected people. This policy explains what data the app processes, why it is needed, and the choices available to you.

Data we process

  • Account information: a Sign in with Apple account identifier and, when Apple provides it, a private relay or account email address.
  • Private pair information: pair membership, hashed and expiring invitation data, and connection timestamps.
  • User content: signal labels, icons, colors, optional short messages, and timestamps that you choose to share.
  • Device and notification information: an APNs push token, platform, app version, and notification preferences needed to deliver alerts.
  • Purchase information: product identifier, transaction identifiers, subscription status, and expiration data supplied through Apple. PulseTogether never receives payment-card details.
  • Operational metadata: delivery state, retry count, and security events needed to operate and protect the service. Private signal text is excluded from logs and analytics.

How we use data

We use this data only for app functionality: authentication, secure pairing, delivering and synchronizing signals, showing history and widgets, managing subscriptions, preventing abuse, responding to support requests, and honoring export or deletion requests. PulseTogether does not sell personal data, show ads, create public profiles, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Service providers and storage

Apple processes Sign in with Apple, push notifications, and App Store purchases under Apple’s terms. Supabase provides authentication, database, and server functions. PulseTogether’s primary Supabase project is hosted in Frankfurt, Germany. Data is encrypted in transit. Access controls restrict pair data to its two current members. PulseTogether does not claim end-to-end encryption.

Notifications and lock-screen privacy

You can disable detailed notification previews in the app or change notification settings in iOS. Widgets mark signal content as privacy-sensitive so the system can hide it while the device is locked, subject to your device settings.

Retention, disconnect, export, and deletion

Account and pair data is retained while your account is active. Disconnecting immediately revokes former-partner access to shared pair data and removes that pair’s shared history. Deleting your account removes your authentication account, pair membership, shared pair history, device registrations, and operational records associated with the account, subject only to short-lived infrastructure backups or records that must be retained for legal and fraud-prevention obligations. Purchase records may remain with Apple.

You can export your data, disconnect, or delete your account from Settings → Privacy in the app. You may also contact us for assistance.

Security and invitations

Invitation codes expire, are protected against enumeration and repeated attempts, and can connect only one other member. Do not share a code publicly. No system is completely secure; report suspected unauthorized access promptly.

Children

PulseTogether is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

We may update this policy when features or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised, and material changes will be communicated where required.
